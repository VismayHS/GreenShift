# GreenShift site: story + Tonight

Scope: the story pages (Persuade, for markers and the viva panel) and the Tonight view (Operate, for the data team), one world.
Audience and job: markers trace claim to evidence; the data team sees tonight's plan and the reason for each delay.
Action: the story's working action is "plan tonight": the live forecast with each job placed in it. Proof: our EDA, energy meter validation, and (when exported) backtest savings. Pending sections say so.
Constraints: offline fallback with a labelled stored forecast; UTC data shown in UK local time; no invented numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Carbon intensity drawn as a synoptic chart: the night's grid has highs and lows, and GreenShift steers each job into the low. Refuses the sustainability-report default (green leaf, hero metric, card row).
OWN-WORLD: pale chart-paper ground, isobar ink navy, brick H for high carbon, deep green L for low carbon, each region its own weather tint (raise from sleeping-city). Contour lines are data, never ornament. One reading size does most of the work (raise from acetate manual).
STORY: understand that the grid's carbon changes by the half-hour and by region; believe the method because every number is sourced; plan tonight.
FIRST VIEWPORT: tonight's national carbon field across 18:00 to 12:00 UK time, drawn as contours filling the screen width, with the L marked and the nightly jobs set on it as departures; headline at the left top; primary action "Plan tonight" opens the Tonight view.
